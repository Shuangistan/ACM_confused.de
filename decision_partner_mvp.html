MVP hypothesis: responsible agentic AI is not achieved merely by placing a human “in the loop”. It requires decision rights calibrated to consequence, uncertainty and reversibility, plus evidence that humans can understand, challenge and override AI appropriately. This prototype intentionally contains a questionable AI recommendation so that appropriate reliance can be tested.
