\section{The problem}

\begin{frame}{What ``human in the loop'' usually means}
  \vspace{1.2em}
  \begin{center}
    {\Large A person reviews each consequential action\\before it takes effect.}
  \end{center}
  \vspace{1.8em}
  \pause
  \begin{center}\color{muted}
    At volume, this is unaffordable --- so it is skipped,\\
    or done at a rate that makes it ceremonial.
    \\[1em]
    And a reviewer shown a recommendation without its reasoning\\
    can do little besides agree.
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Human-centred is not a person in every loop}
  \vspace{1.6em}
  \begin{center}
    {\Large It is choosing the \textbf{unit of human judgement}\\
     so that judgement \emph{scales}.}
  \end{center}
  \vspace{2em}
  \pause
  \begin{center}
    Review the \textbf{rule}, not the case.\\[.6em]
    {\color{muted}A judgement made once is recorded, attributed, and reused.}
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\section{Contribution 1 --- the matrix and the loop}

\begin{frame}{Contribution 1: an executable decision-rights matrix}
  \vspace{.8em}
  Our group's matrix assigns one of four tiers of AI authority from
  \textbf{harm}, \textbf{reversibility}, \textbf{rights} and \textbf{evidence}.
  \vspace{1em}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{ll}
    \toprule
    \texttt{human\_only}         & AI may not decide \\
    \texttt{human\_approval}     & a person approves before it takes effect \\
    \texttt{ai\_with\_oversight} & bounded, logged, reversible \\
    \texttt{ai\_recommends}      & propose; do not act \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
  \vspace{1.2em}
  \pause
  \begin{center}
    Most such artefacts live in a document.\\
    \textbf{This one decides}, in nine lines, in a module that
    \textbf{imports nothing}.
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Two implementations, tested for agreement}
  \vspace{1em}
  In Python on the server; in JavaScript in the page, so the sliders respond
  without a round trip.
  \vspace{1em}
  \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{.55em}
    \item Two copies of the rule allocating oversight can drift with
      \textbf{nothing failing}.
    \item The page would show one tier whilst the server acted on another.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{1.2em}
  \pause
  \begin{center}
    A test extracts the classifier \emph{from the shipped page}, runs it under
    Node,\\ and compares \textbf{all 1{,}000 input combinations}.
    \\[.5em]
    {\color{muted}\small Mutating one threshold: twenty disagreements, each named.}
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The gate asks one question}
  \vspace{.7em}
  \begin{center}\large\emph{Has a person already ruled on this logic?}\end{center}
  \vspace{.9em}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{lll}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Tier} & \textbf{Approved rule fires} & \textbf{Nothing fires} \\
    \midrule
    \texttt{human\_only}         & proceeds & \textbf{\color{brick}waits} \\
    \texttt{human\_approval}     & proceeds & \textbf{\color{brick}waits} \\
    \texttt{ai\_with\_oversight} & proceeds & proceeds, sampled \\
    \texttt{ai\_recommends}      & proceeds & proceeds, sampled \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
  \vspace{1em}
  \pause
  \begin{center}\color{muted}
    The tier sets what is at stake --- not whether a person is asked.
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Why approved logic proceeds even at the top tier}
  \vspace{1.2em}
  \begin{center}{\Large A rule an expert approved \textbf{is their decision.}}\end{center}
  \vspace{1em}
  \begin{center}
    They saw the clause. They accepted that any case matching its body\\
    receives its conclusion. Applying it executes a call they have made.
  \end{center}
  \vspace{1.4em}
  \pause
  \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{.5em}
    \item Asking again for every matching case is how oversight becomes ceremony.
    \item A proportion is audited anyway --- 50\,\% at \texttt{human\_only},
      5\,\% routine, \textbf{never zero}.
    \item Sampling is deterministic on the case: an auditor can recompute it.
    \item Any rule can be retired, which enumerates what it already decided.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Expert review of prepared decisions}
  \vspace{1em}
  The matrix says the AI \emph{prepares} the action and a person rules on it.
  \vspace{.9em}
  \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{.55em}
    \item So the answer \textbf{is} written --- withheld from the person
      waiting, shown to the expert.
    \item In an \textbf{editable} field. An expert who may only accept or
      refuse is not reviewing, they are signing.
    \item What is sent is attributed to them, and says whether they revised it.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{1.3em}
  \pause
  \begin{center}\color{muted}
    Nothing is said to the person waiting in the meantime.\\
    A holding answer gesturing at the outcome \emph{would be} the decision.
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Their judgement becomes logic --- once}
  \vspace{.7em}
  An expert rules. The system drafts the clause their ruling implies, checks it
  fires on the case's own facts, and offers it \textbf{in the same interaction}:
\begin{lstlisting}
not_evict(c) <- in_arrears(c), disputes_debt(c),
                not formal_notice_given(c).
\end{lstlisting}
  \vspace{.6em}
  \pause
  \begin{center}
    Not approved automatically.\\[.4em]
    {\color{muted}They ruled on an \emph{answer}; this is a generalisation of
    that answer,\\ and may be wider than they meant. One further click, with the
    clause in front of them.}
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\section{Contribution 2 --- the logic database}

\begin{frame}{Contribution 2: the logic database as formal substrate}
  \vspace{1em}
  Recording a person's judgement as a rule is only honest if the rule behaves.
  \vspace{1.1em}
  \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{.65em}
    \item \textbf{Determinism} --- function-free Horn clauses, a terminating
      fixpoint, snapshot matching. Same facts, same rules, same answer.
    \item \textbf{Explainability} --- forward chaining, so the derivation
      \emph{exists}. The proof is a transcript, not a story told afterwards.
    \item \textbf{Transparency} --- every rule carries its approver and its
      record; every decision its facts, its rules and its tier; history is
      append-only.
    \item \textbf{Formal footing} --- stratified negation, canonical clause
      identity, safety checked at construction.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Evidence that stays honest}
  \vspace{.6em}
\begin{lstlisting}
will_get_wet(C) <- going_out(C), raining(C),
                   not has_umbrella(C).
\end{lstlisting}
  \vspace{.8em}
  \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{.5em}
    \item Umbrella recorded \textbf{absent} $\rightarrow$ fires.
    \item Umbrella \textbf{unmentioned} $\rightarrow$ does not, and the head is
      \emph{blocked}, not false.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{1.1em}
  \pause
  \begin{center}\color{muted}
    Facts come from a model reading free text. Under a closed world,\\
    silence becomes evidence against a person one round later.\\[.4em]
    {\color{brick}\textbf{We shipped that defect twice in a predecessor.}}
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Near-miss detection and generalisation}
  \vspace{1.1em}
  \textbf{\color{petrol}What almost fired.}
  A clause needing three conditions with two met is not a miss --- it is a
  question, and a precise one:
  \begin{center}\emph{is \texttt{disputes\_debt(c)} the case?}\end{center}
  {\color{muted}\small One fact then settles it by approved logic rather than by
  a model's judgement.}
  \vspace{1.3em}
  \pause
  \textbf{\color{petrol}What a merge would do.}
  Two clauses alike but for one condition merge to their shared core --- replayed
  over every stored case first, and shown with what it keeps, newly decides, and
  contradicts.
  \begin{center}\color{muted}\small
    Never applied automatically: a generalisation fires on strictly more cases
    than its sources.
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\section{Evidence}

\begin{frame}{Failures encountered during development}
  \vspace{.7em}
  \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{.6em}
    \item \textbf{A greeting was classified.} ``Hi'' $\rightarrow$ \emph{human
      approval required}: confidence 0, and the rule reads
      \texttt{confidence < 70}.
    \item \textbf{A gate that overfitted its own prompt.} 6/6 handover on
      ``toner'', 0/6 on ``envelopes'' --- the difference being which word was in
      our example list.
    \item \textbf{The model was the limit, not the prompt.} Four rounds of
      rewording; then we measured. Fact extraction: Haiku \textbf{0/4}, Sonnet
      \textbf{4/4}.
    \item \textbf{Conclusions stored as facts.} The extractor was offered rule
      \emph{heads} as vocabulary and wrote one down as evidence.
    \item \textbf{Silent no-ops.} Three times an edit failed to match and left
      code that looked right and did nothing.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Coverage results}
  \vspace{.7em}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{lrr}
    \toprule
    & \textbf{Run A} & \textbf{Run B} \\
    \midrule
    Decided from approved logic & 2 / 12 & 3 / 12 \\
    Parked for a person         & 5 / 12 & 4 / 12 \\
    \textbf{Coverage}           & \textbf{18\,\%} & \textbf{30\,\%} \\
    Distinct predicates coined  & 19 & 15 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
  \vspace{1em}
  \pause
  \begin{center}\color{muted}
    Twelve cases, empty database, every proposed rule approved.\\
    We report the range, not the better number.
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Why it is not higher}
  \vspace{.6em}
  The vocabulary the database built for itself:
\begin{lstlisting}
reorder_toner          reorder_paper
toner_stock_low        paper_stock_low
\end{lstlisting}
  \vspace{.6em}
  \begin{center}Four terms, two rules, one idea.\end{center}
  \vspace{.9em}
  \pause
  \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{.4em}
    \item Coverage appears where the entity stayed \emph{out} of the predicate
      --- \texttt{in\_arrears}, \texttt{debt\_disputed}.
    \item It fails where the entity went \emph{into} the name.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{.8em}
  \begin{center}\color{petrol}\textbf{
    \texttt{reorder(C, X) $\leftarrow$ stock\_low(C, X).}}\\[.3em]
  {\color{muted}\small The engine is first-order and can express this. Nothing
  proposes it:\\ generalisation can widen a rule, it cannot repair a vocabulary.}
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What we have not measured}
  \vspace{1em}
  \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{.7em}
    \item \textbf{No expert has used this.} Every approval in those runs was
      granted by a script.
    \item \textbf{Two runs is not a measurement.} 18 versus 30 per cent on
      identical input.
    \item \textbf{Top-tier conduct is a prompt, not a mechanism.} It held under
      direct pressure --- three trials is an anecdote.
    \item \textbf{The matrix has defects we did not fix.} Reversibility does
      nothing at harm 4--7; confidence overrides harm entirely. We report them
      rather than quietly amend our group's artefact.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
